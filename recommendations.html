Nutrition & Product Recommendations

The same nutrients Keeper surfaces in the app during Pre-Mom, Mom, and Post-Partum mode — with links to where you can buy them.

Where this comes from

Keeper's Mom and Post-Partum modes already show daily targets for nutrients like folate, iron, choline, and omega-3s, sourced from ACOG, Mayo Clinic, and the CDC. This page lists the same nutrients with two shopping options per category: a general, budget-friendly search on Amazon, and a specific, practitioner-grade product from Thorne for anyone who prefers a more tested, higher-end brand. Food sources always come first in the app — supplements are a backup, not a replacement, and Keeper always says to check with your provider before starting one.

Prenatal multivitamin

Covers folate, choline, iron, iodine, and most other nutrients Keeper tracks in one daily supplement — the first thing ACOG and Mayo Clinic recommend starting before conception.

General option: Search prenatal vitamins on Amazon

Practitioner-grade option: Thorne Basic Prenatal — methylated folate, chelated iron, and choline in one formula.

Iron

Pregnancy roughly doubles iron needs (27 mg/day). Gummy prenatals often fall short, so some people need a separate supplement — Keeper's app content says to ask a provider first.

General option: Search iron supplements on Amazon

Practitioner-grade option: Thorne Iron Bisglycinate — a gentler, well-absorbed iron form.

Choline

Most prenatal vitamins don't include enough choline, and about 90% of pregnant women fall short of the 450 mg/day target Keeper's app cites from ACOG.

General option: Search choline supplements on Amazon

Practitioner-grade option: Thorne choline options

Omega-3 (DHA + EPA)

Keeper's app content covers ACOG's 2–3 servings of fish a week guidance, plus emerging research on DHA+EPA supplementation — most benefit shown in people who test low in omega-3 status, at roughly 1,000 mg combined DHA+EPA a day.

General option: Search prenatal omega-3 (DHA+EPA) on Amazon

Practitioner-grade option: Thorne Prenatal DHA — 650 mg DHA + 200 mg EPA per serving.

Vitamin D

Works with calcium for fetal bone development; Keeper's app notes many people run low and a provider can check blood levels.

General option: Search vitamin D on Amazon

Practitioner-grade option: Thorne vitamin D options

Vitamin B12 (vegan/vegetarian)

Keeper's app flags B12 as a must-discuss supplement for anyone who doesn't eat animal products, both during pregnancy and while breastfeeding.
