Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy describes what Keeper actually does today, written by its developer (Mettlark LLC). It has not yet been reviewed by an attorney — treat it as accurate but not yet finalized for legal purposes.

The short version

Keeper is a period, pregnancy, and postpartum tracking app that runs entirely on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and no company database anywhere holding your information. Your cycle logs, symptoms, moods, journal entries, and voice notes are stored only in Keeper's local data file on your device. Mettlark LLC, the company behind Keeper, cannot see, access, sell, or share your data — not because we promise not to, but because we have no technical way to receive it in the first place.

Information Keeper does not collect

  • No accounts. There's no sign-up, login, username, or email address collected anywhere in the app.
  • No server. Keeper has no backend that any version of the app talks to. There is nothing for Mettlark LLC to store, breach, subpoena, or sell data from.
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs. Keeper does not include any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising software development kits, and does not track your usage of the app.
  • No cookies in the app. The app itself sets no cookies and makes no tracking requests.

Information stored only on your device

Keeper stores the following locally, using Apple's on-device data framework (SwiftData). None of it is transmitted anywhere by Keeper:

  • Cycle, period, and fertility-window dates you log or that Keeper calculates from your logs
  • Symptom and mood tags you choose to log
  • Journal entries and voice note recordings you create, and any on-device transcript generated from them
  • Pregnancy, postpartum, and birth-control tracking information you enter
  • Basal body temperature, read from Apple Health if you grant permission (see "Apple Health" below)

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Keeper reads your basal body temperature from Apple Health to help fill in your fertility log. Keeper never writes any data back to Apple Health, and this permission is entirely optional — Keeper works without it.

Voice notes and transcription — the one narrow network exception

If you record a voice note and tap Transcribe, newer iPhones transcribe the recording entirely on-device using Apple's Speech framework. On older iPhones that lack on-device transcription support, iOS itself may send just that audio clip to Apple's servers to perform the transcription. This is a feature of Apple's operating system, not something Mettlark LLC built, can see, or can turn off — and it is the only network call anywhere in the Keeper app, and only happens if you choose to tap Transcribe.

Backups

The only copies of your data anywhere besides your phone are ones you control directly: a backup file you export yourself, or your phone's standard iCloud device backup (if you have that turned on in iOS Settings) — encrypted under your own Apple ID, the same as a photo or a note on your phone. Mettlark LLC has no access to either.

Face ID, Touch ID, and the discreet app icon

Keeper can lock itself behind your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode, and you can optionally switch its home-screen icon to a plain, generic one. Both are local device features; neither sends any information anywhere.

"How common is this" statistics

When Keeper shows that a symptom is common, that figure always comes from a published medical source (such as ACOG or the CDC) cited in the app — never from other Keeper users' data. Keeper has no way to see, combine, or compare your logs with anyone else's, because it has no server to do that comparison on.

Deleting your data

A single button in Keeper's Settings permanently deletes every log stored on your device, immediately — there is no waiting period, because it's your data on your own phone. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data (any device backup containing older data remains under Apple's standard backup rules until you also delete that backup).

This website and affiliate links

This website (keeperhq's support site) is a static page and does not itself collect personal information or set tracking cookies, beyond what's required to load a web font from Google Fonts. Some pages — including the Nutrition & Product Recommendations page — contain links to third-party retailers (currently Amazon, Thorne, and Perelel). Some of these are or will be affiliate links: if you buy something after clicking one, Mettlark LLC may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Once you click through to a third-party site, that site's own privacy policy and data practices apply — Mettlark LLC does not control, and is not responsible for, what those companies do with your information.

Children's privacy

Keeper does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because it collects no personal information at all — everything stays on the device it's logged on. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Mettlark LLC through this website or in connection with the app, contact us at the email below and we will address it.

State and regional privacy laws (California, Washington, and others)

Some states — including Washington's My Health My Data Act and California's CCPA/CPRA — give you specific rights over health and consumer data, such as the right to know, delete, or stop the sale of your information. Because Keeper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal or health information with Mettlark LLC or anyone else, there is generally no data for these rights to act on. If you have questions about how a specific law applies to Keeper, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

If Keeper's practices change — for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud-sync feature — this policy will be updated first, with a new "Last updated" date above, before that change ships.
