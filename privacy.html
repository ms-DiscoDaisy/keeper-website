Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Written by Keeper's developer (Mettlark LLC) to describe, factually, what the app and this website actually do. It has not yet been reviewed by an attorney — in particular, whether on-device-only storage legally counts as "collection" under laws like the CCPA/CPRA and Washington's My Health My Data Act is a specific open question flagged for that review, not a settled legal conclusion.

Interpretation and Definitions

Definitions

  • Company ("the Company," "We," "Us," "Our") refers to Mettlark LLC.
  • Service refers to the Keeper application and this website.
  • Application refers to Keeper, the software program provided by the Company.
  • Device means any device used to access the Service, such as an iPhone.
  • Personal Data ("Personal Information") is information that relates to, or could reasonably be linked with, an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Business, Consumer, CCPA, and CPRA have the meanings given to them under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.
  • You means the individual using the Service.

What Keeper stores on your device

Keeper stores the following using Apple's on-device data framework (SwiftData), in a data file that lives only on your device:

  • Cycle, period, and fertility-window dates you log or that Keeper calculates from your logs
  • Symptom and mood tags you choose to log
  • Journal entries, voice note recordings, and any on-device transcript generated from them
  • Pregnancy, postpartum, and birth-control tracking information you enter
  • Basal body temperature, read from Apple Health if you grant permission (see "Apple Health" below)

The Company does not receive, access, store, or have any technical means to view this information. It is written to local storage on your device by the app running on your device, and it stays there unless you choose to move it — by exporting a backup file, or through your device's own iCloud backup, both of which are described below and are entirely under your control, not the Company's.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Keeper reads your basal body temperature from Apple Health to help fill in your fertility log. Keeper never writes any data back to Apple Health, and this permission is optional. Like everything above, this data is read and stored on your device only.

Voice notes and transcription — the one exception where data can leave your device

If you record a voice note and tap Transcribe, newer iPhones transcribe the recording entirely on-device, using Apple's Speech framework. On older iPhones that lack on-device transcription support, iOS itself may send just that audio clip to Apple's servers to perform the transcription. This is a feature of Apple's operating system, which the Company did not build, cannot see the contents of, and cannot turn off. It is the only circumstance in which any information from the Keeper app travels off your device, and it only happens if you choose to tap Transcribe.

Information you send Us directly

If you email the Company (for example, at the support address below) with a question or feedback, We receive your email address and whatever you choose to write — this is information We actually do receive, unlike the device-stored information above. We use it only to respond to you, and keep it only as long as reasonably needed to do that and to handle any follow-up.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It does not set tracking cookies and does not run any analytics script. Two things happen automatically that are worth disclosing plainly:

  • Our host (GitHub Pages) may log basic technical request information (such as IP address and browser type) the way any web server does — this is standard web-hosting infrastructure, not something the Company configures, accesses, or analyzes.
  • This site loads a web font from Google Fonts, which involves your browser making a request to Google's servers for that font file.

Some pages — including the Nutrition & Product Recommendations page — link to third-party retailers (currently Amazon, Thorne, and Perelel). The Company is not currently a participant in any affiliate advertising program with these or any other company. If that changes, this policy and those links will be updated first to disclose it clearly.

Backups

The only copies of your device-stored data anywhere besides your phone are ones you create or control directly: a backup file you export yourself, or your phone's standard iCloud device backup if you have that turned on in iOS Settings — encrypted under your own Apple ID. The Company has no access to either.

Deleting information

A button in Keeper's Settings permanently deletes every log stored on your device, immediately. For anything you've sent Us directly by email, contact Us at the address below and We will delete it from Our records.

Disclosure for legal reasons

The Company may disclose information in the good-faith belief that doing so is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a valid request from a public authority, protect the Company's rights, investigate wrongdoing in connection with the Service, or protect the safety of users or the public. Because the Company does not hold your device-stored data, there is generally nothing of that kind for it to disclose.

Security

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, including the security protections built into your own device and Apple's platform. The Company relies on those platform protections (Face ID/Touch ID lock, iOS's own device encryption) rather than maintaining separate security measures of its own, since it holds no copy of your data to separately secure.

CCPA/CPRA Privacy Notice (California Residents)

This section supplements the rest of this Privacy Policy and applies to California residents.

Categories of Personal Information

The categories below are those defined in the CCPA/CPRA. For each, We describe what actually happens, rather than a bare yes/no, since the "collected" framing doesn't map cleanly onto information that only ever exists on your own device:

  • Category A — Identifiers (e.g. email address): Received by the Company only if You email Us directly. Not collected any other way.
  • Category B — California Customer Records (includes medical information): The health and personal-tracking information described in "What Keeper stores on your device" above falls in this category, but it is stored only on Your device and is never received, accessed, or stored by the Company.
  • Categories C, D, E, G, H, I, J, K, L (protected classifications, commercial information, biometric information, geolocation, sensory data, employment information, education records, inferences, and other sensitive personal information): Not received or stored by the Company in any circumstance.
  • Category F — Internet or network activity: Not collected by the Company beyond the standard web-hosting request logs described under "This website" above, which the Company does not access or analyze.

Sources, use, and disclosure

The only Personal Information the Company receives comes directly from You, and only if You choose to email Us. We use it solely to respond to you. The Company does not disclose Personal Information to any third party for a business or commercial purpose, and has not done so in the preceding twelve months.

Sale or sharing of Personal Information

The Company has not sold or shared Personal Information of California residents, as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and does not do so.

Your rights under the CCPA/CPRA

California residents have the right to notice, to know/access what Personal Information has been collected, to correct it, to delete it, to limit use of sensitive Personal Information, to opt out of sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise any of these rights, contact Us at the email below. Because the Company holds so little Personal Information to begin with (see above), most requests will be straightforward to resolve. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days, extendable once by another 45 days if reasonably necessary, consistent with the CCPA/CPRA.

Minors

The Company does not knowingly collect Personal Information from anyone under 16, does not sell it, and would require parental authorization before doing so if that ever changed.

"Do Not Track" (CalOPPA)

This Service does not respond to Do Not Track browser signals. Some third-party websites linked from this Service may track browsing activity; you can control this through your own browser's privacy settings.

California's "Shine the Light" law

Under California Civil Code § 1798.83, California residents can request information about a business's sharing of Personal Data with third parties for those parties' own direct marketing. The Company does not disclose Personal Information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

California minors' content-removal rights

California residents under 18 may request removal of content they've posted publicly through the Service by contacting Us with the email address they used. Note that Keeper has no public posting features today, so this would apply only if that changes in the future.

Children's and minors' privacy generally

The Service is not directed to children, and the Company does not knowingly collect Personal Information from anyone under 16 through the Service — because, as described throughout this policy, it does not collect Personal Information from anyone, of any age, except what you choose to send by email. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided information to the Company this way, contact Us and We will delete it.

State and regional health-data laws (Washington, and others)

Some states, including Washington's My Health My Data Act, give specific rights over consumer health data. Whether information that only ever exists on Your own device, and that the Company never receives, falls within these laws' definitions of "collection" or "processing" is a question this policy's attorney review is specifically intended to confirm, rather than something this document asserts as settled. If you have questions about how a specific law applies to Keeper, contact Us.

Links to other websites

The Service may link to websites not operated by the Company, including the medical and research sources cited in the app. The Company has no control over, and is not responsible for, the content or privacy practices of any third-party site. Review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Changes to this policy

If Keeper's practices change — for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud-sync feature, or the Company is accepted into an affiliate program — this policy will be updated first, with a new "Last updated" date above, before that change takes effect.
