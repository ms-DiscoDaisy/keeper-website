Keeper

A 100% on-device period, pregnancy & postpartum tracker — no accounts, no server, nothing leaves your phone.

What Keeper does

Keeper tracks periods, fertility, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, and birth control, and shows educational content alongside that tracking — sourced from ACOG, CDC, Mayo Clinic, and Cleveland Clinic. Everything runs on your own device. There are no accounts to create and no server collecting your data.
